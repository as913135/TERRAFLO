TerraFlow maps India's critical energy transmission pipelines (GAIL HVJ, Urja Ganga), petroleum product corridors (IOCL, BPCL), and municipal bulk water supply networks (Delhi Jal Board, Bengaluru Cauvery Grid, Mumbai Vaitarna Mega-Tunnels, Narmada Potable Grid). Integrated with real-time multi-factor AI risk prediction and device push alerts.

National Security & Safety Advisory — TerraFlow calculates failure vulnerability, excavation strike probability & inspection priority across cross-country hydrocarbon and municipal water corridors. Access is restricted to verified operators and engineers. In-line inspection (PIG) and non-destructive potholing are mandatory prior to any mechanical excavation.
